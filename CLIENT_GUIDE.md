# MyShop - Customer & Business Guide

A simple, easy-to-use online shopping platform designed to make buying and selling straightforward.

---

## What is MyShop?

MyShop is an online store where customers can browse and purchase products, and your business can manage inventory, process orders, and keep everything organized in one place.

---

## For Customers: How to Shop

### Getting Started
1. **Create an Account** - Sign up with your email and password
2. **Browse Products** - Search or filter by category, price, or product tags
3. **Add to Cart** - Click "Add to Cart" on any product
4. **Checkout** - Review your order, enter delivery address, and pay
5. **Track Order** - Check your order status anytime

### Product Shopping Features
- **Search** - Find products by name or keyword
- **Filter & Sort** - Filter by category, price range, or what's on sale
- **Product Details** - See full description, images, price, and stock status
- **Related Products** - Discover similar items
- **Save Favorites** - Browse items without buying
- **Stock Information** - Know if items are available before adding to cart

### Cart & Checkout
- **Manage Cart** - Add/remove items or change quantities anytime
- **Free Shipping Alert** - See when you qualify for free shipping
- **Shipping Estimate** - Know the delivery cost before paying
- **Saved Addresses** - Store multiple delivery addresses
- **One-Click Checkout** - Fast repeat purchases

### Orders & Tracking
- **Order History** - See all your past and current orders
- **Real-Time Status** - Track where your order is:
  - **Order Received** - We've processed it
  - **Being Prepared** - Staff is packing it
  - **In Transit** - On the way to you
  - **Delivered** - Your order arrived
- **Order Details** - See items, price breakdown, and delivery address
- **Search Orders** - Find by order number or amount
- **Delivery Timeline** - Visual indicator shows your order progress

### Payments
- **Secure Payment** - Pay safely through Paystack
- **Payment Verification** - Automatic confirmation when payment completes
- **Multiple Payment Methods** - Card payments accepted
- **Clear Pricing** - No hidden fees

### Communications
- **Message Support** - Chat with the store directly in the app
- **Message History** - see all conversations
- **Read Status** - Know if your message was seen
- **Send Attachments** (if available) - Share photos or documents

### Notifications
- **Order Updates** - Get notified when status changes
- **Chat Alerts** - Know when you receive a message
- **Promotions** - Hear about new sales or products
- **Browser Notifications** - Opt-in to desktop alerts
- **Filter by Type** - View only important notifications

### Account Management
- **Profile** - Update your name, email, and password
- **Addresses** - Store and manage delivery locations
- **Order History** - View past purchases and spending
- **Notification Settings** - Control what alerts you receive
- **Logout** - Securely sign out

## Common Questions

### "Is my payment data safe?"
Yes. All payments are processed through Paystack, a trusted payment processor. We never store your complete card details.

### "How long does delivery take?"
Delivery time depends on your location. You'll see the estimated delivery cost at checkout. Our team will begin processing immediately after payment confirms.

### "What if I need to cancel my order?"
Contact support through the messaging feature or by phone as soon as possible. We can usually cancel if the order hasn't started shipping.

### "Can I change my delivery address?"
Once payment is complete, the address is locked to prevent delivery issues. Contact support for special requests.

### "How will I know my order arrived?"
You'll receive a notification when the status changes to "Delivered." You can also check your order page anytime.

### "What if something goes wrong?"
Use the in-app messaging feature to contact the store, or call our support team. We're here to help!
