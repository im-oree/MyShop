❓ Pricing FAQ

Common Questions

What's included in the price? +
Our pricing includes the complete MyShop platform setup, customization to your business, and initial launch. Each plan includes a fully functional online store, payment processing, and customer accounts. We handle all the technical setup so you can focus on selling.
Are there extra monthly costs? +
The MyShop setup is a one-time fee. After that, you'll have small ongoing costs for hosting your store (usually $10-50/month depending on your traffic) and payment processing fees from Paystack. No hidden fees or surprise charges.
Can I upgrade later? +
Yes! Start with Starter and upgrade to Professional later. We can add analytics, staff management, messaging, or any other features you need as your business grows. Just let us know and we'll add it on.
Can you customize it for my specific needs? +
Absolutely. MyShop is designed to be completely flexible. Whether you need custom features, special integrations, unique payment methods, or anything else — we can build it. The Enterprise plan covers custom development and we'll work with you to create exactly what you need.
